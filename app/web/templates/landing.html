What it checks

Email protection

Stops others sending mail as you.

  • SPF who may send email as you
  • DMARC what happens to forged mail
  • DKIM signed email, common selectors

Encryption and HTTPS

Keeps visits private and trusted.

  • TLS certificate valid, trusted, not expiring
  • TLS versions no outdated protocols
  • HTTPS and HSTS HTTP redirects to HTTPS

Website hardening

Browser protections and certificate controls.

  • Security headers CSP, framing, referrer and more
  • Cookie flags Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite
  • CAA who may issue certificates

What it cannot do

  • It cannot see inside your systems. It does not check MFA, backups or patching, and it does not map to the Essential Eight.
  • DKIM "not detected" only means not found under the common names we try. Your provider may use a different one.
  • It does not find subdomains or scan ports.
  • This is a learning project. Treat results as a starting point, not a full security assessment.
